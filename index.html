Fingertip to tooltip, one catalog

Cylinders, valves, controls. One record per part.

Bailey makes and distributes hydraulic cylinders, power units, valves and electronic controls, and sells them to OEMs, through distributors and at retailers like Tractor Supply. The same cylinder has to be described correctly in every one of those places. This page is a short look at how Salsify keeps that straight.

Why it matters now

A brand consolidation is a product content project

Unifying Sure Grip and Hydrolico under Bailey means every title, image, spec sheet and part number on every channel needs to reflect it. Doing that by hand, per channel, is exactly the kind of work that stalls. Doing it from one record is a mapping change.

Technical attributes

Bore, stroke, rod diameter, pressure rating, mount type: a cylinder is defined by numbers, and every channel wants them in its own fields.

Retail and distribution

A Chief cylinder on Tractor Supply needs a consumer-friendly title and image set. The same part at a distributor needs a drawing and a cross-reference.

Custom and standard

Custom-engineered products and stock parts share components and should share content.

What Salsify does

Channel-specific publishing

One record, different output per destination: titles, bullets and image sets tailored by channel rules.

What a product change looks like with Salsify

1. Change onceA product manager updates a spec, image or description in Salsify, or it flows in from the ERP or engineering.
2. WorkflowThe right approvers sign off in their queue. Rules apply automatically.
3. ValidateReadiness reports check the record against every destination's current spec and image rules.
4. PublishOne click sends the correct format to OEM portals, distributors, retailers and baileyhydraulics.com.
5. MeasurePage grades and compliance scores come back into Salsify so the team sees what landed.
